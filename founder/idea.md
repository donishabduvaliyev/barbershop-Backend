# SmartChair (Barbershop & Grooming OS)

- **What it is**: An integrated barbershop operating system and customer booking platform featuring a zero-download Telegram Web Mini App, cross-platform mobile apps for iOS/Android, and a real-time management dashboard for shop owners.
- **Who it is for**: 
  - **Primary Buyers (B2B)**: Independent barbershop owners, salon managers, and solo master barbers in Tashkent, Uzbekistan seeking to eliminate phone call disruptions, reduce appointment no-shows, and automate staff commission tracking.
  - **End Consumers (B2C)**: Urban male clients (ages 18–45) who want a guaranteed haircut time slot with their favorite barber in under 20 seconds directly inside Telegram or via a dedicated native mobile app.
- **What it sells, at what price**: 
  - **B2B SaaS Subscription**: 200,000 UZS (~$18 USD) per shop per month after a 14-day free trial. Includes unlimited bookings, multi-staff calendar sync, walk-in quick register, automated Telegram reminder notifications, and revenue analytics.
  - **B2C Client Booking**: 100% free for consumers to search, book, review, and rebook.
- **Where and how**: 
  - **Location**: Tashkent, Uzbekistan initially, expanding to Samarkand, Bukhara, and national metro areas.
  - **Distribution / Customer Acquisition**:
    1. **Hyper-local In-person Onboarding**: Founder visits high-density barbershop hubs in Tashkent, provides an instant 5-minute setup on the owner's phone/tablet, and delivers custom acrylic QR code mirror stickers for walk-in client capture.
    2. **Digital Paid Acquisition**: Short-form Instagram Reels & Telegram channel video ads showcasing the contrast between chaotic paper notebooks/phone calls vs 1-tap Telegram booking.
  - **Delivery**: Cloud SaaS hosted on dedicated VPS with MongoDB, WebSockets for real-time schedule updates, and Telegram Bot Webhook integration.
- **Budget and constraints**: 
  - Production-ready full-stack software already engineered (backend, web mini app, mobile app, admin panel).
  - Low cloud overhead (~$50/month fixed server costs).
  - High reliance on Telegram in the target geography (Telegram is Uzbekistan's primary digital communication and commerce rails).
