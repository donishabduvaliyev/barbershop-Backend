## 1. Top Cross-Cutting Risks (Raised by Multiple Board Members)

1. **Barber Chair-Side Adoption & Inertia (Offers + Product)**:
   - *The Danger*: Shop owners buy the software, but individual master barbers ignore the app during busy shifts, leading to desynchronized schedules, double-bookings, and client frustration.
   - *Action*: Keep the chair-side view zero-friction: 1-tap walk-in toggle and instant sound chimes that pierce hair clippers.
2. **Platform Leakage / Disintermediation (Offers + Monopoly)**:
   - *The Danger*: Clients book the first cut through SmartChair, then take the barber's phone number directly to bypass the system.
   - *Action*: Build an automated retention loop that pings the client on Telegram 21 days after their haircut with a 1-tap re-book button before their barber's Saturday schedule fills up.
3. **Payment Collection & Churn Friction (Offers + Monopoly)**:
   - *The Danger*: At the end of the 14-day free trial, if barbers must do manual bank transfers, renewal rates will plummet.
   - *Action*: Connect local Uzbek payment rails (Click Merchant / Payme Business) for automatic monthly debit card renewals.

## 2. Integrated Pre-Launch Conditions Checklist

- [ ] **Condition 1 (Retention Loop)**: Implement an automated Telegram bot reminder sent 18–24 days post-haircut (*"Hi [Name], it's been 3 weeks since your cut with [Barber]. Tap to reserve your spot this week"*).
- [ ] **Condition 2 (Physical Distribution)**: Produce branded acrylic QR code mirror stickers for every chair in onboarded shops, turning walk-in cash clients into repeat Telegram bookers.
- [ ] **Condition 3 (Local Payments)**: Integrate Click / Payme / Uzum auto-renewing subscriptions for the 200,000 UZS/month fee.
- [ ] **Condition 4 (White-Glove Photography)**: Capture 5 professional photos per shop for the first 25 barbershops to anchor the catalog to high-end aesthetic standards.
- [ ] **Condition 5 (Fast Walk-In Entry)**: Ensure the shop dashboard allows barbers to block a chair for an unscheduled walk-in in under 2 seconds.

## 3. The Strongest Version of This Business

The board sees SmartChair not merely as a booking calendar, but as **the digital operating system and identity rail for the modern Uzbek grooming market**. In its ultimate form, SmartChair dominates the top 200 barbershops in Tashkent, captures 100,000 urban men on Telegram, and handles the entire salon lifecycle: appointments, barber commission splits, automated client retention, and integrated payments. Because Telegram is the default social fabric of Uzbekistan, SmartChair can build a regional monopoly before foreign incumbents can adapt.
