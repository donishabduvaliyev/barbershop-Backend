# Board Brief: SmartChair

## The Business
SmartChair is a vertical SaaS and consumer marketplace for the barbershop and male grooming industry in Uzbekistan (launching in Tashkent).

### The Product Suite
1. **Telegram Web Mini App**: Runs natively inside Telegram with zero installation. Clients open the bot, choose a shop, select their barber, pick an available time slot, and receive instant booking confirmation.
2. **Native Mobile App (iOS / Android in React Native)**: Complete client app featuring interactive maps (Leaflet/CartoDB), favorite shops, booking history, ratings, and instant re-booking.
3. **Shop Owner Dashboard**: Tablet/desktop admin panel providing live calendar slots, quick walk-in appointment entry, staff commission payouts, client CRM, and shop profile management.
4. **Real-time Backend**: Node.js/Express, MongoDB, Socket.io for instant schedule sync across chairs, and Telegram Bot webhook integration for push notifications.

### Market & Geography
- **Primary Market**: Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Population ~3 million; estimated 2,500+ barbershops and salons.
- **Cultural Fit**: In Uzbekistan, Telegram is ubiquitous (>85% penetration). People use Telegram for news, messaging, payments, and shopping. Telegram Mini Apps have near-zero user onboarding friction.

### Monetization & Pricing
- **Pricing**: 200,000 UZS (~$18 USD) per shop/month.
- **Free Trial**: 14 days full access with zero credit card commitment.
- **Consumer Cost**: Free forever.

### Go-To-Market / Distribution
1. **Direct In-Person Sales**: Founder walking into barbershops in key Tashkent districts (Chilanzar, Yunusabad, Mirzo Ulugbek) demonstrating the app on iPad, onboarding staff in 5 minutes, and providing free branded QR mirror stickers.
2. **Digital Social Video**: High-engagement Instagram Reels and Telegram influencer channels showing barbers managing appointments effortlessly with phone in hand.

### Technical & Capital Position
- Working production code already built and tested.
- Fixed monthly server expenses are ~$50 USD.
- Variable cost per shop is ~$2.50 USD (database/server capacity and SMS gateway costs).
